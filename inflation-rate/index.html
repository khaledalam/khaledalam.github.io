About the data. “Last” is the most recent official figure: consumer prices compared with the same month (or quarter) a year earlier. For each country it comes from whichever of these publishes the newest figure: the IMF CPI dataset, the OECD, the BIS, the World Bank Global Economic Monitor, or the national central bank (Venezuela). Where none has a figure from the past year, “Last” is the latest annual average, marked “avg”.

The history, averages and forecasts use annual average inflation: the IMF World Economic Outlook from 1980, and the World Bank for earlier years and gaps. Years from this year onward are IMF estimates and projections and are marked as such. New figures are checked for automatically every day.

Source: IMF WEO / World Bank.
